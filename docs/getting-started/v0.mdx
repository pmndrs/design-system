[Open in v0](https://ui.shadcn.com/docs/registry/open-in-v0) ignores `css` and
`cssVars`. Our [`v0` item](https://pmndrs.github.io/design-system/r/v0.json)
supplies `app/globals.css` with the shadcn and MD3 Tailwind mappings instead.

Its [`palette.json` dependency](https://pmndrs.github.io/design-system/r/palette.json)
supplies the shared light and dark colours, imported once:

```css title="app/globals.css"
@import "../src/assets/pmndrs.css";
```

Both registry URLs follow `main`.
